From San Francisco to Washington by road, in eight zones: the fuel burnt according to the speed, the crashes from 130 km/h up, the cars that chase, the truck that takes three bombs and the map between zones. The title from the ROM's bytes, checked against openMSX; the eight zones, the map, the crashes, CHECK POINT and GOAL in GIF, and the four tunes from its own engine, identical to openMSX frame by frame.

Casio - (c) 1985 - 16 KB cartridge at 0x8000 - MSX - Dump e93c3966...

There are no screenshots of the game here. The logo and the title come from running the ROM (checked against openMSX), and the GIFs are the ROM running in openMSX: on every frame the VRAM and the VDP registers are logged, and our own code paints them.

What is inside

The game

  • The game: the controls, the fuel, the crashes and the points, as the code has them.
  • The zones: the map of the journey, the eight zones, CHECK POINT and GOAL, in GIF.
  • The other cars: the cars that chase, the truck and what it drops, the shots and the bombs.
  • The sprites: the car, the other cars and the truck, hit and blowing up, the bomb and the loose things, put together from the ROM tables.
  • The texts: every text in the ROM, the names of the cities and CASIO from the four sides.
  • The music: the four tunes, from its own sound engine and checked against openMSX.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: the zeros that stay, a tune nobody asks for, the letters at a fixed address and the code nobody reaches.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, the BIOS it uses, the interrupt, the road in RAM and the tools.
